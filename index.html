Getting around

Bus

Strætó 50 to Reykjavík

From the Þjóðbraut stop to Mjódd in Reykjavík in about 45 minutes, roughly every 1–2 hours. Buy tickets in the Klappið app.

Timetables on straeto.is
Car

Reykjavík in 45 minutes

Through the Hvalfjörður tunnel (no toll since 2018). Keflavík airport is about 1 h 20 min. Check wind warnings before crossing Kjalarnes.

Road conditions on umferdin.is
Sea

Tides matter here

The tide range is up to 4 m. Langisandur beach is at its widest at low tide; Breiðin point is best for watching the surf.

In town

Beach & hot pool

Langisandur & Guðlaug

A long golden sand beach, unusual in Iceland. Guðlaug is a geothermal pool built into the sea wall, warm water with the ocean right below.

Lighthouse

Akranesviti

You can climb to the top for a view across Faxaflói to Reykjavík and Snæfellsjökull on clear days. The older lighthouse beside it is from 1918.

Museum

Byggðasafnið í Görðum

The Akranes Folk Museum: fishing boats, old houses and everyday life on the peninsula. Winter: Saturdays only.

Swimming

Jaðarsbakkalaug

The town pool by the beach, with hot tubs. Open until 22:00 on weekdays.

Hike

Akrafjall

The mountain behind town. Háihnúkur (555 m) is the usual goal, 2–3 hours return from the car park off road 51.

Check wind before you go
Food

Galito & Gamla Kaupfélagið

Galito for dinner in town; Gamla Kaupfélagið serves a hot weekday lunch. Krónan and Bónus have everything else.

Day trips nearby

Glymur waterfall

40 min

One of Iceland's highest waterfalls, at the end of Hvalfjörður. A steep 3–4 hour hike with a log crossing; the log is removed in winter.

Borgarnes Settlement Centre

30 min

The story of Iceland's settlement and Egil's Saga, a good rainy-day stop.

Deildartunguhver & Krauma

1 h

Europe's most powerful hot spring, with Krauma baths right next to it.

Reykjavík

45 min

By car through the tunnel, or bus 50 to Mjódd and then city buses.

Snæfellsnes peninsula

2 h

Kirkjufell, black beaches and the Snæfellsjökull glacier. A full day; start early in winter.

Good to know

  • Tap water is excellent, and the hot water smells a little of sulphur. That is normal.
  • At swimming pools, shower without a swimsuit before going in. It is the rule, and staff will remind you.
  • Cards work everywhere, even for small amounts. Tipping is not expected.
  • Alcohol to take away is sold only at Vínbúðin, not in supermarkets.
  • Weather changes fast. Look at the wind forecast, not just the temperature.
  • Northern lights season is September to April; the cloud forecast on vedur.is tells you where the sky is clear.
